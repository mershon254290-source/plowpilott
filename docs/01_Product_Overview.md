# FlowPilot — Product Overview

## 1. Product Definition

FlowPilot is an AI-powered business process automation consultant.

It analyzes manual business processes, identifies automation opportunities, evaluates automation potential, recommends AI agents, and proposes where human involvement should remain in the workflow.

The product is designed to help businesses answer a fundamental question:

> **Which of our manual processes should we automate first, and how should we automate them?**

---

## 2. The Problem

Businesses perform many operational processes manually.

Common examples include:

* HR onboarding
* Invoice processing
* Customer support
* Data entry
* Document processing
* Employee administration
* Sales operations
* Reporting
* Email-based workflows
* Spreadsheet-based processes

These processes often involve repetitive tasks, multiple systems, manual decisions, and frequent communication.

As a result, businesses experience:

* Repetitive work
* Human errors
* Slow processing
* Operational bottlenecks
* Increased labor costs
* Inconsistent execution
* Limited visibility into automation opportunities

The problem is not simply a lack of automation tools.

The bigger problem is:

**Businesses often do not know what to automate, why they should automate it, or how an automated process should be designed.**

---

## 3. The Solution

FlowPilot acts as an intelligent automation consultant.

A user describes an existing business process using natural language.

FlowPilot analyzes the process and produces a structured automation assessment.

The assessment includes:

1. Process summary
2. Task identification
3. Task classification
4. Automation opportunities
5. Automation score
6. AI agent recommendations
7. Human-in-the-loop recommendations
8. Suggested automation workflow

This transforms an unstructured business description into an actionable automation plan.

---

## 4. Target Users

FlowPilot is initially designed for businesses that rely heavily on manual operational work.

Potential target segments include:

### Small and Medium Businesses

Companies that use:

* Email
* Excel
* Documents
* CRM systems
* HR systems
* Accounting systems
* Internal databases

and want to identify automation opportunities without hiring a large automation consulting team.

### Operations Teams

Operations managers can use FlowPilot to identify repetitive workflows and prioritize automation projects.

### HR Teams

HR departments can analyze processes such as:

* Employee onboarding
* Document collection
* Employee data entry
* Account creation requests
* Notifications

### Finance and Accounting Teams

Potential processes include:

* Invoice processing
* Data entry
* Document verification
* Approval workflows
* Reporting

### Automation Consultants

Consultants can use FlowPilot to accelerate the discovery and assessment stage of automation projects.

---

## 5. Core Value Proposition

FlowPilot helps organizations move from:

**Manual Process**

to

**Automation Assessment**

to

**AI Agent Design**

to

**Automated Workflow**

Instead of asking:

> "What AI tool should we use?"

FlowPilot starts with the business process and asks:

> "What should be automated, and what is the best way to automate it?"

This process-first approach is central to the product.

---

## 6. Core Product Capabilities

### Process Analysis

FlowPilot analyzes a natural-language description of a business process.

### Task Extraction

The process is broken into individual operational tasks.

### Task Classification

Tasks are classified according to their nature, such as:

* Data Entry
* Communication
* Validation
* Decision
* System Update
* Input
* Other

### Automation Assessment

Each task is evaluated based on its potential for automation.

### Automation Score

FlowPilot produces an overall automation score that helps prioritize processes.

### AI Agent Recommendations

The system identifies where AI agents could perform useful roles.

Examples include:

* Document Processing Agent
* Data Entry Agent
* Validation Agent
* Communication Agent
* Notification Agent

### Human-in-the-Loop

FlowPilot identifies situations where human review or approval should remain part of the process.

This is important for processes involving:

* Approvals
* Exceptions
* Sensitive decisions
* Unclear information
* Business judgment

### Workflow Design

FlowPilot proposes a high-level automated workflow showing how tasks could interact with AI agents, systems, and human users.

---

## 7. Product Philosophy

FlowPilot follows three principles.

### Process First

Understand the business process before selecting technology.

### Automation With Human Control

Not every task should be fully automated.

Human oversight should remain where it provides meaningful value.

### Practical Automation

The goal is not to automate everything.

The goal is to identify automation that produces measurable business value.

---

## 8. Current Product Status

FlowPilot is currently an MVP.

The MVP supports:

* Natural-language process input
* AI-powered process analysis
* Task extraction
* Task classification
* Automation scoring
* AI agent recommendations
* Workflow recommendations
* Human-in-the-loop identification

The current implementation uses Python, Streamlit, Ollama, and a local Qwen model.

---

## 9. Product Vision

The long-term vision is to evolve FlowPilot from an automation assessment tool into an intelligent business automation platform.

The expected evolution is:

```text
Process Discovery
       ↓
Process Analysis
       ↓
Automation Assessment
       ↓
Automation Blueprint
       ↓
AI Agent Design
       ↓
Workflow Generation
       ↓
Workflow Execution
       ↓
Monitoring & Optimization
```

The ultimate goal is to help organizations discover, design, deploy, and continuously improve AI-powered business workflows.

---

## 10. Strategic Direction

The initial commercial strategy is to use FlowPilot as an automation assessment and consulting accelerator.

A potential customer journey is:

```text
Customer
   ↓
Describe Manual Process
   ↓
FlowPilot Assessment
   ↓
Automation Report
   ↓
Automation Blueprint
   ↓
```

---

## 11. Success Criteria

The product should eventually help a business answer five questions clearly:

1. What parts of this process are manual?
2. What parts are repetitive?
3. What should be automated?
4. Where can AI agents help?
5. Where should humans remain involved?

If FlowPilot can answer these questions accurately and consistently, it provides a useful foundation for business automation.
